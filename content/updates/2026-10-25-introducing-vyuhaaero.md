VyuhaAero Systems is an Indian aerospace and autonomous-systems venture based in Chennai and incubated at V-NEST, VIT Chennai.

This website is where we will share our progress. We will only publish what we can stand behind: our work is at an early stage, and we describe it by the evidence behind it.

Start with the launch film on the home page. It sets out the problem we are working on and the direction we are taking.
